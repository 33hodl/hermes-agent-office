# Design Brief — Theme 3: "Dunder Mifflin" (The Office, US)

> Sources: RoomSketcher 3D floor plan tour, Arnold's Office Furniture layout analysis, The Office fandom wiki. Tasteful show-fan details, not memes — a believable Scranton branch where Hermes agents happen to work.

## Look & feel
- **Warm, fluorescent-lit, 2000s paper-company office** — beige walls, gray-blue carpet, mustard accents, clutter. Slightly worn-in and lived-with; cozy but corporate.
- Same isometric world; furniture reads as real office furniture (cubicles, filing cabinets, CRT-ish monitors on desks).

## Palette
- Walls: beige/cream `#d9c9a8` / `#e3d5b8`
- Carpet: gray-blue `#8b93a8` / `#7d8599`
- Wood: mid-brown desk tops `#a3764a`, dark filing `#5b4a38`
- Accents: mustard `#d9a441`, Dunder Mifflin blue `#3b5d8c` (logo-ish), paper white `#f4f1e8`
- Break room: vending machine red `#b03a2e`
- Characters: office-worker beans — muted shirt tones (white, light blue, gray, mustard), some with ties

## Layout (Scranton branch, faithful arrangement)
1. **Reception** (front): curved front desk (Pam's post), guest chairs, coat rack, plant, filing shelves
2. **Bullpen**: clusters of 6x6 cubicles — sales desks; Jim & Pam desks across from each other; Dwight's desk by the window with a beet plant
3. **Michael's office** (corner): wood desk, guest chairs, globe, green walls, horizontal blinds — "Regional Manager" placard
4. **Conference room**: long wooden table + chairs, whiteboard easel, TV on stand, blinds
5. **Break room**: round table, vending machine, coffee maker, fridge
6. **Annex**: smaller workstations (5x5), broom-closet office (Ryan's)
7. **Warehouse** (far side): shelves, boxes — the "delivery" drop point = reception inbox / mail cart
- Windows along walls with blinds; ceiling fluorescent strips

## UI elements
- Panels styled as office paper: lined notepads / manila folders / whiteboard
- Roster panel: "DUNDER MIFFLIN — SCRANTON BRANCH" header, agents as employees ("Jim", "Dwight", "Pam"... themed default demo names optional)
- Metrics: "TODAY'S OUTPUT" (tokens), "EMPLOYEES WORKING"
- Mail widget: "INBOX" — manila envelope icon, unread badge
- Agent modal: employee badge card (name, role = session source, status), activity plan as task list on a notepad, loadout as "supplies"
- Delivery toast: "New mail for Michael Scott" vibes → "New delivery in your inbox"
